FAQ

Answers before you buy

Is this a subscription or a perpetual licence?

It is a perpetual (one-time) licence for Office 2024 Home, not a Microsoft 365 subscription. Microsoft’s terms on activation, devices and updates still apply.

Which apps are included?

Word, Excel and PowerPoint for PC or Mac, per Microsoft’s Home 2024 offer. Outlook and other business apps are not part of this edition.

Can I use it offline?

Yes—with desktop apps installed you can work offline; periodic connectivity and licence checks may still apply under Microsoft’s rules.

How do I activate after purchase?

Go to setup.office.com, sign in with your Microsoft account, enter the email key and follow the wizard. Then install apps from office.com.

Who is Office 2024 Home for?

Home and personal use. If you need Outlook or more professional scenarios, consider Office 2024 Home & Business.
