FAQ

Answers before you buy

What’s the difference between Office 2024 and Microsoft 365?

Office 2024 Home & Business is a one-time perpetual licence for 1 PC or Mac. It does not include typical Microsoft 365 extras (1 TB OneDrive, Copilot, Defender). Microsoft 365 is a subscription with ongoing updates, cloud and AI.

Does the Office 2024 licence expire?

No—perpetual ESD licence. You pay once and keep the apps on the device where you installed them, under Microsoft’s terms.

Can I install it on multiple devices?

No—typically 1 PC or Mac only. For more devices, consider Microsoft 365 Personal or Family.

How does it differ from Office 2024 Home?

Home & Business adds Outlook (and OneNote) for light professional use, still as a perpetual licence.

How do I activate?

Use setup.office.com with the emailed key, link your Microsoft account and install from office.com.
